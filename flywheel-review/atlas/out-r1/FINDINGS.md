## 4. Limits

- The Atlas checks that it copied the ledgers faithfully. It does not re-run any number.
- Tools, versions and pins are read from free text with a small grammar; 274 of 355 instrument strings are pointers or keep parts as notes.
- `law N` and `failure mode N` are found by pattern. A sentence such as "law 40 and 123" is ambiguous and is reported, not guessed.
- Failure classes (K1 to K11) and themes (A to J) are chapter 02's grouping, graded Likely there.
- The namespace is a placeholder (`.example`), as x3d.ML's is. No file is signed; the receipt binds results to file hashes only.
